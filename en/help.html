HOW TO RESTART

Make it easier to pick things up.

Save, take a break, and begin again at your own pace.

01

Name your adventure

Games, books, drawing, studying—give any part of your day a save point.

02

Save before a break

Leave a short note about your progress, your next step, and anything worth remembering.

03

Continue when you are ready

Even after some time away, your earlier notes will show you where to begin again.
